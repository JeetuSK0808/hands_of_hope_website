## Mission & Vision (final — safe to use verbatim)

**Mission:** Hands of Hope is change powered by high school students, who run a network of branches, each one mastering a single cause, and all of them uniting every spring to pack thousands of care kits for the communities they've served across the U.S. and internationally.

**Alt / tagline version** (hero banner, bio, or social use — not the formal mission): Hands of Hope is proof that one student mastering one cause can change a community — multiplied across a growing network of youth-run branches around the world.

**Vision:** A world where compassion doesn't wait for adulthood, where students lead real change in the causes they've mastered, and every community has people who stick with them, not just people who show up once.

## The Two-Goal Model
Everything HOH does serves two goals at once:
1. **Global impact** — reaching communities around the world with real, sustained support
2. **Student growth** — turning high schoolers' compassion into leadership experience and hands-on action

These aren't two separate programs — Goal 1 is what happens when enough branches do Goal 2 well. HOH doesn't scale by launching "global initiatives," it scales one branch, one student, at a time.

## The Branch System — Depth Over Breadth
- HOH is a network of independent branches, each based at a school or in a community, each going deep on ONE cause instead of spreading across many.
- This is the real differentiator: a typical single-cause nonprofit's *entire* mission is often just one HOH branch's full-time focus. The founding branch — Innovation Academy — is actually two specialized sides running as one:
  - **STEM Together**, which volunteers with MDE School and other special needs schools
  - **Atlanta Mission**, which helps sustain the day-to-day operations of Atlanta Mission, an understaffed local organization

  Innovation Academy is the one branch built this way — two causes instead of one, a reflection of its status as the founding branch. Every other branch runs a single cause. (The STEM Together model also runs at a second branch elsewhere in the org.)
- Branches choose their own cause, community partners, and programming. The autonomy is real, not nominal.

## What Unites the Branches
- **Ripple for Change (Spring)** — once a year, every branch comes together to pack thousands of care kits built around a single theme. The theme isn't a guess: throughout the year, branches track what the communities they've served say they need most, tally the responses, and build that year's kits around whatever comes out on top. This is the moment "many branches" becomes visibly "one organization."
- **Awards Ceremony (Winter)** — recognizes branches and individual members for the year's work.

## Leadership Does the Work Too (read before writing any copy)
The most important rule for this site: HOH's leadership — co-founders, executives, branch leaders — are not administrators positioned above the volunteer work. They ARE the volunteers. Founders pack kits at Ripple for Change. Branch leaders run their programming hands-on instead of delegating it away.

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "HOH empowers students to lead branches" | "HOH is students — leadership included — running branches" |
| "Leadership trains and coordinates branches" as the main story | "Leadership runs branches and does the work alongside every member" |
| Org chart framed as a management hierarchy | Org chart framed as a division of hands-on responsibility |

Quick test for any sentence: if the subject is "HOH" or "leadership" and the object is "students," rewrite it so students — leadership included — are the ones doing the verb.

## Voice & Tone
- First person plural ("we"), active voice
- Verbs to lead with: pack, run, lead, build, show up, volunteer
- Verbs to avoid as the *headline* action: empower, enable, facilitate, oversee (fine as supporting language, not as the main verb)
- Skip generic nonprofit phrasing ("make a difference," "changing the world one step at a time") — name the actual thing instead (kits, branches, Ripple for Change)
